# Nordlicht Plus — Policy Document (version 2026.1)

Insurer: Nordlicht Health (fictional). Plan code: NP-PLUS. Monthly premium: EUR 340. All amounts are in EUR.

## S1 Definitions

**Calendar year**: 1 January to 31 December.
**Deductible**: the amount you pay each calendar year for covered services before the plan starts to share the cost. Copays do not count toward the deductible.
**Copay**: a fixed amount you pay for a covered service.
**Coinsurance**: the percentage of the allowed amount you pay after the deductible has been met.
**Out-of-pocket maximum**: the most you pay in a calendar year for covered services. Premiums do not count toward it.
**In-network provider**: a provider contracted by Nordlicht Health. **Out-of-network provider**: any other provider.
**Prior authorization**: approval from Nordlicht Health that is required before certain services are covered.

## S2 Eligibility and enrollment

You are eligible if you live in Germany and are at least 18 years old. Your spouse or partner and your children up to age 25 can be added as dependents.
Coverage starts on the first day of the month after we receive your application and your first premium. You can change plans during open enrollment each November.
If a premium is not paid, a grace period of 30 days applies. Coverage continues during the grace period, but claims may be held until the premium is received.

## S3 How cost sharing works

Your annual deductible is EUR 800. Your annual out-of-pocket maximum is EUR 3500.
Services with a copay are charged at the fixed copay and are not subject to the deductible. All other covered services are subject to the deductible first, then to coinsurance.
For in-network providers you pay 15 percent coinsurance after the deductible. For out-of-network providers you pay 40 percent and may also be billed the difference between the provider's charge and the allowed amount.
Once you reach the out-of-pocket maximum, the plan pays 100 percent of covered in-network services for the rest of the calendar year.

### S3.1 Worked examples

Example 1: You have not paid anything toward your deductible this year and have an in-network lab panel with an allowed amount of EUR 60. The whole EUR 60 counts toward your deductible and you pay EUR 60.
Example 2: Your remaining deductible is EUR 100 and you have an in-network imaging service with an allowed amount of EUR 480. You pay EUR 100 for the deductible plus 15 percent of the remaining EUR 380, which is EUR 157.00.
Example 3: A specialist visit costs a copay of EUR 40 however much the provider charges, and the copay does not reduce your deductible.
Example 4: You have already reached your deductible. An in-network service with an allowed amount of EUR 90 then only costs you the coinsurance percentage of EUR 90.

### S3.2 Order of payment

Each claim is processed in the following order: (1) check that the service is covered and any prior authorization is in place; (2) determine the allowed amount; (3) apply the copay or the deductible; (4) apply coinsurance; (5) check whether the out-of-pocket maximum has been reached. Your EOB shows the result of each step.

## S4 Covered benefits

### S4.1 Preventive care

Annual check-ups, recommended screenings and vaccinations are covered at 100 percent at in-network providers. The deductible does not apply.
Preventive visits are limited to one routine check-up per calendar year. Additional visits for symptoms are treated as GP visits.

### S4.2 GP and specialist visits

A GP visit has a copay of EUR 15. A specialist visit has a copay of EUR 40. A referral from your GP is required for specialist visits, except dermatology skin checks once per calendar year.
Visits to out-of-network providers are subject to the out-of-network coinsurance in S3 instead of the copay. Telephone consultations with your GP are charged as a GP visit.

### S4.3 Hospital stays

Inpatient stays are subject to the deductible and then coinsurance. Prior authorization applies where listed in S5. Emergency admissions do not need prior authorization.
A hospital stay includes room, nursing care, surgery and the attending doctors' services. Private room upgrades are not covered.

### S4.4 Emergency care

An emergency room visit has a copay of EUR 100. Emergency care is covered worldwide. See S7 for the notification rule for out-of-network hospitals.
If you are admitted to hospital directly from the emergency room, the emergency copay is waived. Urgent care that is not an emergency is charged as a specialist visit.

### S4.5 Prescription drugs

Generic drugs have a copay of EUR 5 per prescription. Brand-name drugs on the formulary have a copay of EUR 25. Specialty drugs require prior authorization.
Drugs not on the formulary are not covered unless your doctor obtains an exception in writing. Over-the-counter drugs are not covered.

### S4.6 Diagnostic imaging and laboratory tests

Imaging and laboratory tests are subject to the deductible and then coinsurance. Prior authorization applies where listed in S5.
The allowed amount for imaging and laboratory services is the contracted rate. Providers may not bill you more than the allowed amount if they are in network.

### S4.7 Physiotherapy

Physiotherapy is covered with a written referral from a physician, up to 12 sessions per calendar year. Sessions are subject to the deductible and then coinsurance.
A new referral is needed for each new condition. Sessions are counted from 1 January regardless of when you enrolled.

### S4.8 Mental health

Outpatient psychotherapy is covered up to 20 sessions per calendar year with a referral from a GP. A copay of EUR 40 applies per session.
Telehealth sessions count toward the session limit. Inpatient psychiatric care is treated as a hospital stay.

### S4.9 Maternity

Maternity care is covered after a waiting period of 9 months (see S6). Routine prenatal visits are covered at the specialist copay.
Delivery in hospital is treated as an inpatient stay. Newborns are covered from birth if you notify us within 30 days.

### S4.10 Dental

Dental treatment is covered at 80 percent after a waiting period of 6 months (see S6), up to EUR 1000 per calendar year. Routine check-ups are covered at 100 percent once the waiting period has passed.
Crowns, bridges and dentures are covered at the same percentage. Costs above the annual limit are paid by you.

### S4.11 Vision

An allowance of EUR 200 per calendar year applies toward glasses or contact lenses. An eye test is covered once every two years.
The allowance can be used once per calendar year. Unused allowance does not carry over.

## S5 Prior authorization

The following services need prior authorization: MRI scans, elective surgery, inpatient stays, specialty drugs.
Your provider or you can request approval at least 5 business days before the service. An approval is valid for 90 days. Without approval, the plan may deny the claim and you may have to pay the full cost.

## S6 Waiting periods

Dental benefits: 6 months from the start of coverage. Maternity benefits: 9 months. Orthodontics: 24 months. All other benefits have no waiting period.

## S7 Exclusions and limitations

The plan does not cover: cosmetic procedures without medical need; experimental or unproven treatments; treatments received outside Germany except emergency care; and services that are not medically necessary.
Physiotherapy beyond 12 sessions per calendar year, and any physiotherapy without a written referral, is not covered.
Brand-name drugs are covered only when no generic equivalent exists. If a generic exists and you choose the brand-name drug, you pay the full price difference in addition to the copay of EUR 5.
Emergency care at an out-of-network hospital is covered at in-network rates only if the member notifies the plan within 48 hours of admission. Otherwise out-of-network cost sharing applies.
Treatment of pre-existing conditions is covered, except that dental and maternity waiting periods still apply.

## S8 Claims and billing

In-network providers send claims directly to us. If you submit a claim yourself, you must do so within 12 months of the service date.
You will receive an Explanation of Benefits (EOB) for every processed claim. An EOB is not a bill. In-network providers may not bill you for more than the allowed amount (no balance billing).
If you believe you were charged twice for the same service, contact the provider first and then us with your EOB.

## S9 Appeals and complaints

You can appeal a denial within 60 days of the date on the denial letter. Send your appeal in writing with your member ID and any supporting documents. We decide within 30 days. If you disagree, you can request an external review.

## S10 Privacy and your data

We process your data to administer your plan, as described in our privacy notice. You have the right to access, correct and delete your data and to object to processing under the GDPR. Claims data is kept for 10 years as required by law.
Health data is special category data. We only share it with providers and service partners to the extent needed to process your claims.

## S11 Coordination of benefits and other coverage

If you are covered by another plan, we coordinate benefits so that the total paid does not exceed the allowed amount. Tell us about any other coverage when you enroll and when it changes.
If a third party such as an insurer in an accident case is liable for your costs, we may recover what we paid from that party.

## S12 Ending your coverage

You can cancel at the end of any calendar year with three months' notice. We can end your coverage for non-payment after the grace period or for false statements in your application.
Claims for services received while you were covered can still be submitted within the filing period in S8.

## S13 Contact and service

Member service is available on weekdays. For urgent medical questions always contact your doctor or the emergency services; member service cannot give medical advice.
